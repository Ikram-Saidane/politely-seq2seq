The dataset

Generated by a reproducible script from hand-written situations and phrasings. Impolite means blunt, impatient or dismissive, never insulting. Splits never share content, and one test set uses phrasings the model never saw.

    How it works

    1. PreprocessNormalise Unicode, lowercase, split into words and punctuation marks.
    2. Vocabulary1,805 entries: 1,801 words seen at least twice in training plus <PAD> <SOS> <EOS> <UNK>.
    3. RepresentEach word id becomes a 128-dimensional embedding vector.
    4. EncodeAn LSTM reads the sentence and produces one hidden state per word.
    5. AttendAt each step the decoder scores every input state and builds a weighted context vector.
    6. DecodeAn LSTM writes the polite sentence one word at a time, from <SOS> until <EOS>.
    7. EvaluateBLEU, chrF, exact match, content kept, politeness (own classifier), success rate.

    Limits worth knowing

    • The training data is synthetic: everyday workplace and service situations, written from templates. Sentences far from these situations are rewritten less reliably — the “Hand-written” results in the Performance tab measure exactly that.
    • English only, one sentence (or two short ones) at a time, up to 40 tokens.
    • Politeness is judged by a small bag-of-words classifier trained on the same data: a useful indicator, not a human judgement.
